# Greenfield Public School (fictional): admissions knowledge base

## Admission process
Admissions for the 2027-28 academic year open on 1 November 2026. The steps are: enquiry, a campus visit with a counsellor, the online application, a short interaction with the child (no written test below Grade 2), and an offer letter. Seats are confirmed after the first term fee is paid.

## Age criteria
Nursery: 3 years as on 1 June 2027. LKG: 4 years. UKG: 5 years. Grade 1: 6 years as on 1 June 2027, as per the state guideline.

## Curriculum and board
The school follows the CBSE curriculum from Grade 1 to Grade 12. Nursery to UKG follow a play-based early years programme. Languages offered: English, Kannada, Hindi; French from Grade 6.

## Fees
Fees are shared by the admissions counsellor after the campus visit, with the full fee structure in writing. There is a one-time registration fee of Rs 1,000 at the time of application.

## Transport
School buses cover Marathahalli, Whitefield, Bellandur, HSR Layout, Sarjapur Road and Kadubeesanahalli. Transport is optional and charged separately by route.

## Timings
School hours are 8:30 am to 3:15 pm, Monday to Friday. Nursery to UKG end at 12:30 pm. Saturdays are for clubs and parent meetings.

## Campus visits
Campus visits run Monday to Saturday, 10 am to 1 pm, and take about 45 minutes. Parents can book a slot with the admissions counsellor.

## Documents
Birth certificate, the child's Aadhaar (optional), two passport photos, the previous school's report card and transfer certificate (Grade 2 and above), and address proof.

## Day care and meals
After-school day care is available until 6:30 pm on weekdays. Lunch and an evening snack are provided for day care students; nutritious vegetarian lunch is available for all students on request.
